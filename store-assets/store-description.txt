Ethereal Violet Theme dresses the Chrome window in a quiet evening palette. The frame sits in deep plum, the toolbar and active tab lift one shade lighter, and the new tab page opens on midnight violet with soft lilac text. Links and accents carry a light orchid tone that stays calm against the dark surfaces. Every layer is a flat, single solid color, and the contrast is tuned so tab titles, toolbar icons and address bar text stay comfortable to read.
